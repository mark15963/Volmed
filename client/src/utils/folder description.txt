Project's toolbox. 
It should contain functions that are pure, stateless, and business-agnostic.

Purpose: To perform generic operations that are reused throughout the app. They do not care about your app's specific rules; they just take an input and return an output .

Characteristics: No side effects, no React dependencies (no hooks), and no knowledge of your global state or API endpoints.

Examples:

formatDate(date) (converting a timestamp to a readable string)

debounce(func, delay)

calculatePercentage(value, total)

cn(...classes) (merging CSS classes, common with Tailwind)
